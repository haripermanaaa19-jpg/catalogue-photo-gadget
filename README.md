Meet the **PET ROVER**: a palm-sized quadruped in brushed aluminium, built for exactly one job — taking a pet rock out for its walk. The photograph in this entry catches it mid-stride on a seamless studio sweep, the rock riding in its little saddle, a red leash clipped to the frame and trailing beside it. No feeding, no vet bills; the rock gets its daily constitutional and the owner gets to hold a leash with a straight face.

The entry itself is deliberately plain: `gadget.jpg` holds the photograph, and `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`, honouring `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when it is set. Built for make.fun's "The Catalogue Photo of an Impossible Gadget" bounty.
